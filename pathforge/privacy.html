Privacy notice

Last updated 27 October 2026

This notice explains what PathForge CAM collects on this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist or ask for the free STL test pack, we collect your name and email address and, if you give them, your country, chosen plan and machine.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the test pack you asked for.
  • To contact you when PathForge CAM is ready to download or buy.
  • To understand which machines and plans people need.

We use your details only because you agreed to it when you joined. We don't sell them or share them for advertising.

Where it is stored

Sign-ups are stored with Supabase, our database provider. The website runs on Vercel. If you send your details by WhatsApp instead, they are kept in our WhatsApp chats.

Security check

To keep robots from flooding our forms, they use Cloudflare Turnstile. It checks that you're a person and receives technical information from your browser, such as your IP address, for that purpose only.

The software itself

PathForge CAM runs on your computer. Your STL files and G-code are not uploaded to us.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until we stop running the waitlist.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by sending us a message on WhatsApp. We reply within 14 days.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Visitor statistics

We count page visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally. It tells us things like how many people visited and which pages they read.
