From STL to the machine in four steps

Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.

  1. Import

    Open an STL from any CAD program. Assemblies split into parts you can click and rotate.

  2. Set up

    Tool size, feeds, stepdown, stock, and where zero is: top or bottom, corner or centre.

  3. Generate

    Roughing, exact waterline finishing of the walls, then a finishing pass over tops and floors.

  4. Check and save

    The program is cut on a virtual block. You save it once the check passes.

The check reads the finished G-code

It replays the exact file your controller will run, on a block cut into 0.1 mm squares, and stops four kinds of mistake.

  • Gouges

    Any move that cuts into the part, measured to the thousandth of a millimetre.

  • Rapid crashes

    A G0 move that would travel through stock that hasn't been cut yet.

  • Cutting too deep

    Moves below the bottom of the part, into your vise, fixture or table.

  • Overloads

    The tool buried deeper than one layer across a wide area. These are the cuts that snap end mills.

What it doesn't do yet

So you know before you buy.

  • 3-axis only

    No 4th or 5th axis machining. You can tilt the part before cutting.

  • Flat end mills only

    Ball-nose tools are planned. A finer finish stepover gives smoother curves.

  • Windows only

    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

  • Dry run every new job

    The check is a simulation. Run each new program in the air first with the feed turned down.

Questions

Will it work with my machine?

PathForge CAM writes G-code for Tormach PathPilot, Haas, GRBL and Mach3/Mach4 controls. Most 3-axis mills and routers use one of these. If yours isn't listed, tell us when you join the waitlist.

Do I need the internet to use it?

No. It runs on your computer. Your files are never uploaded.

Which files can I import?

STL files, both binary and text, from any CAD program.

Who can use the Academic plan?

Students and teachers, for learning and non-commercial projects.

What does "one year of updates" mean?

Your licence works forever. New versions released in the first year after you buy are included at no extra cost.

How will I pay?

Payment options will be announced to the waitlist before sales open.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If PathForge CAM doesn't work for your machine, ask within 14 days of buying for a full refund. See the refund policy.
