About PathForge CAM

PathForge CAM was built to make CNC toolpath generation simple and reliable, without the cost or complexity of enterprise CAM software. It's tuned for Tormach machines and built by someone who actually runs the machines it generates code for.

The Academic license exists so students and educators can learn real CNC workflows for free. Personal and Business licenses fund ongoing development and support for makers and shops who rely on it daily.

Support & FAQ

Which CNC machines does PathForge support?

PathForge is tuned specifically for Tormach machines, and its G-code output is broadly compatible with other CNC mills that accept standard G-code. If you're unsure about your specific controller, reach out before buying.

What's the difference between the license tiers?

Academic is free with limited multi-axis strategies, for learning and coursework. Personal unlocks every strategy for a single seat. Business adds multi-seat deployment for shops and teams. Full breakdown is in the Pricing section above.

Do I need an internet connection to use it?

No. PathForge runs fully offline once installed and licensed. An internet connection is only needed to activate your license the first time.
