Refund policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

We want you to be sure before you pay, and safe if it doesn't work out.

14-day refund

If PathForge CAM doesn't work for your machine, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying and you get your money back in full.

How to ask

  • Send us a WhatsApp message with your order reference and the email you used to buy.
  • Tell us briefly what didn't work. It helps us fix it for others, but you don't have to.

What happens next

  • We reply within 2 working days.
  • The money goes back the way you paid, usually within 10 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Your licence key stops working once the refund is made.

The Academic plan

The Academic plan is free, so there is nothing to refund.
