About PathForge CAM

PathForge CAM is made by an independent developer for people who run CNC mills and routers.

Why it exists

Most CAM mistakes are found the expensive way: a broken end mill, a ruined part or a crash. PathForge CAM was built around one idea: the software should check its own G-code before it ever reaches your machine.

How it is tested

  • Every release makes 100 test programs: 5 test shapes, 5 strategies and 4 machine controls.
  • Every program is replayed on a virtual block and checked for gouges, rapid crashes, cuts that go too deep and tool overloads.
  • A release only ships when all 100 pass. The deepest cut into a test part today is 0.002 mm, against a limit of 0.005 mm.

You can run the same tests yourself with the free STL test pack.

What it doesn't do yet

PathForge CAM is 3-axis only, works with flat end mills and runs on Windows. Ball-nose tools are next. We'd rather tell you now than after you've paid.
